<p class="lede">A Verified badge means we checked a firm's credentials and insurance and kept proof on file. It is earned, never bought.</p>

We're verifying our first providers now. Until a firm's checks are complete, it appears as listed, not verified.

## What we check, by category

<div class="table-scroll" markdown="1">

| Category | Checked before the Verified badge |
| --- | --- |
| Senior move managers | NASMM membership or an equivalent credential, and current insurance |
| Aging life care managers | Aging Life Care Association membership and professional certification |
| Estate sale companies | Current insurance, a written contract with every client, their commission range published on the profile, and no upfront fees |
| Senior specialist realtors (coming soon) | An active Georgia real estate license and the SRES designation |
| Cleanout crews and movers (coming soon) | Current insurance, and Georgia mover certification where it applies |

</div>

## How a check works

1. The firm claims its free profile.
2. The firm sends its evidence, such as an insurance certificate, membership or license.
3. We check it with the issuing association or state lookup where one exists, and keep a copy on file.
4. A person reviews the file and approves the badge.
5. We re-check when insurance expires and at least once a year.

## When a badge comes off

We remove a badge if insurance lapses, a credential can't be confirmed, a firm misrepresents itself, or a complaint shows it no longer meets these standards.

## What verification doesn't mean

Verification confirms credentials and insurance at the time we check. It isn't a guarantee of any firm's work. Always get a written contract and ask questions before you hire.
