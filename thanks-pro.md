## What happens next

<ol class="steps-list">
  <li>We ask for the evidence we check for your category, such as an insurance certificate, membership or license.</li>
  <li>We check it with the issuing association or state lookup where one exists, and keep a copy on file.</li>
  <li>A person reviews the file and approves the Verified badge.</li>
</ol>
