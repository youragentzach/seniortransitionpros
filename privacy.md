## Who we are

Senior Transition Pros is a directory and referral service for Atlanta families. <mark class="confirm">[CONFIRM: legal entity name.]</mark> You can reach us at [hello@seniortransitionpros.com](mailto:hello@seniortransitionpros.com).

## What we collect

**From families who request a shortlist:** your name, email or phone number, city or suburb, your situation, your timeline, how you found us, and anything else you choose to tell us.

**From providers who claim a profile:** firm name, contact name, work email, phone number, the services you offer, the suburbs you serve, and the documents you send for verification.

We don't ask for financial account numbers, medical records or government ID numbers, and you shouldn't send them.

## How we collect and store it

Our forms are processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form service, which delivers each submission to our email inbox. <mark class="confirm">[CONFIRM: email provider, and where intake data is stored if that changes.]</mark>

## How we use it

- To follow up with you and understand what you need.
- To match a family with providers, and to share the family's details with the providers the family approves.
- To verify providers and keep their profiles accurate.
- To improve the site and our guides.

## Who we share it with

- **The providers you approve.** We tell you exactly who received your request.
- **Services that run the site:** GitHub Pages hosts the site, FormSubmit processes forms, and our email provider stores messages. They handle data only to provide those services.
- **When the law requires it.**

We never sell your information, and we never share a family's details with a provider the family hasn't approved.

## Cookies and tracking

We don't use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this site. Our hosting and form services may record standard technical data, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure their services.
