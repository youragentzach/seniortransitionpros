## What money never buys

Short lists are based on what the family needs, credentials and reviews, whether or not a firm pays. Founding Pro doesn't change who we recommend to families, and badges, reviews and awards can't be bought. Read [how we make money](/how-we-make-money/).

## How verification works

<ol class="steps-list">
  <li>You claim your free profile.</li>
  <li>You send your evidence, such as an insurance certificate, membership or license.</li>
  <li>We check it with the issuing association or state lookup where one exists, and keep a copy on file.</li>
  <li>A person reviews the file and approves the badge.</li>
  <li>We re-check when insurance expires and at least once a year.</li>
</ol>

What we check depends on your category. See the full standard on [how we verify](/how-we-verify/).
